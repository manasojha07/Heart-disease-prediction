Know More About Health Risk Factors

📊 Age

Enter your current age in years. Age is a significant factor in heart disease risk assessment.

⚥ Gender

Select your biological gender. Risk factors and disease presentations can vary between genders.

⚖️ BMI (Body Mass Index)

A measure of body fat based on height and weight. Calculate it using the formula: weight (kg) / height (m)². Normal range: 18.5-24.9

🚭 Smoking

Smoking is a major risk factor for heart disease. It damages blood vessels and increases the risk of blood clots.

🍷 Alcohol Consumption

Moderate alcohol consumption may have protective effects, but excessive drinking increases heart disease risk significantly.

🏃 Physical Activity

Low: Minimal exercise (less than 150 minutes/week)
Moderate: Regular exercise (150-300 minutes/week)
High: Active lifestyle (more than 300 minutes/week)

🥗 Diet Quality

Poor: High in processed foods, sugars, and saturated fats
Average: Balanced but occasional unhealthy choices
Good: Rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins

😴 Sleep Hours

The recommended sleep duration for adults is 7-9 hours per night. Inadequate sleep increases heart disease risk.

😰 Stress Level

Chronic stress can contribute to heart disease. High stress levels can lead to unhealthy coping behaviors and increased blood pressure.

🩺 Blood Pressure

Enter your systolic blood pressure (the top number). Normal: below 120, Elevated: 120-129, High: 130 or higher.

💓 Resting Heart Rate

Your heart rate when at rest (typically 60-100 bpm). Athletes may have lower resting heart rates (40-60 bpm).

💢 Chest Pain

Chest pain or discomfort can be a warning sign of heart problems. If you experience frequent chest pain, consult a healthcare provider.

😮‍💨 Shortness of Breath

Difficulty breathing, especially during light activities, can indicate heart or lung problems.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family History

A family history of heart disease, especially in close relatives at a young age, increases your risk significantly.
